A place to sit down properly

Posh Cuisine and Lounge sits on a quiet stretch of road in Owerri, behind glass frontage that opens onto a bright, tiled dining room. Booths line one wall, tables fill the floor, and the counter at the back keeps a steady rotation of bread, meat pies and pastries under warm light.

The kitchen leans into rice dishes done properly, soups made the way they are meant to be made, and a cake counter that draws people in on its own. Whether it is a quick pastry on the way past or a full sit down meal with people you like, the room is built to hold both.
